We work with industry, government agencies, research institutions, and academic partners to address complex engineering and infrastructure challenges through AI-enabled modeling, optimization, uncertainty analysis, and decision-support tools.

Our collaborations may include applied research projects, technical studies, computational model development, research software, professional training, and expert engagement aligned with the capabilities of the A-SMART Lab and the policies of the Singapore Institute of Technology.

## Areas of Expertise

### AI and Data-Driven Engineering

Machine learning, scientific machine learning, surrogate modeling, engineering analytics, large language model applications, AI-assisted simulation, validation, and interpretability.

### Optimization and Decision Support

Mathematical and multi-objective optimization, uncertainty quantification, sensitivity analysis, scenario analysis, risk assessment, and techno-economic analysis.

### CCUS and Low-Carbon Infrastructure

CO₂ pipeline and maritime transport, integrated capture-transport-storage networks, infrastructure reuse, hydrogen infrastructure, and regional decarbonization planning.

### Subsurface and Geoenergy Analysis

Geological CO₂ storage, geothermal systems, underground hydrogen storage, reservoir modeling, injectivity, storage performance, leakage, and subsurface risk.

### Research Software and Computational Tools

Research-grade computational prototypes, scientific Python workflows, simulation and optimization tools, model integration, visualization, and reproducible workflows.

### Training and Knowledge Exchange

Technical workshops, professional training, seminars, and knowledge exchange in AI-enabled engineering, optimization, CCUS, and subsurface systems.

## How We Can Work Together

- **Collaborative research:** Joint projects addressing strategic engineering challenges.
- **Sponsored applied research:** Defined studies developed around an organization’s technical needs.
- **Technical feasibility studies:** Assessment of technologies, infrastructure configurations, and operating strategies.
- **Research software prototyping:** Computational models, analytical workflows, and proof-of-concept tools.
- **Training and workshops:** Customized technical learning and knowledge exchange.
- **Student engagement:** Industry-supported FYPs, capstone projects, internships, and team projects.
