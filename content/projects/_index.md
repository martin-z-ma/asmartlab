# Current Projects

### Risk-Adjusted Techno-Economic Analysis of Cross-Border CO₂ Maritime Transport

<div style="margin-left: 24px;">

<p>This project develops an integrated framework for evaluating <strong>Singapore-linked cross-border CO₂ maritime transport and storage pathways</strong>. The research combines process modeling, techno-economic analysis, uncertainty quantification, risk analysis, and machine learning to assess uncertainties across liquefaction, buffer storage, port operations, shipping, receiving terminals, and storage interfaces.</p>

<p>The project aims to support more robust infrastructure planning by identifying the technical, economic, and operational factors that most strongly influence project cost, risk, and long-term viability.</p>

<p><strong>Funder:</strong> Ministry of Education Academic Research Fund (MOE AcRF) Tier 1<br>
<strong>Role:</strong> Principal Investigator<br>
<strong>Funding:</strong> S$150,000</p>

<p><strong>Research themes:</strong> CCUS · Maritime CO₂ Transport · Techno-Economic Analysis · Uncertainty · Risk-Informed Decision-Making</p>

</div>

### AI-Assisted Integrated Verification & Tracing System for Rebar Delivery

<div style="margin-left: 24px;">

<p>This project develops <strong>AI-enabled and data-driven technologies for end-to-end verification, traceability, and visibility across the rebar supply chain</strong>.</p>

<p>The research integrates digital records, automated verification, data analytics, and intelligent decision-support methods across manufacturing, logistics, delivery, and construction operations. The goal is to improve traceability, reduce manual verification effort, strengthen quality assurance, and support more efficient project delivery.</p>

<p><strong>Funder:</strong> Industry Alignment Fund – Industry Collaboration Project<br>
<strong>Role:</strong> Project Co-Lead<br>
<strong>Funding:</strong> S$1.4316 million</p>

<p><strong>Research themes:</strong> Artificial Intelligence · Digital Traceability · Construction Technology · Data Analytics · Industry 4.0</p>

</div>

# Selected Previous Projects

### SimCCS: Development and Applications

<div style="margin-left: 24px;">

<p>Development and application of <strong>SimCCS</strong>, an integrated open-source platform for designing and evaluating CO₂ capture, transport, and storage infrastructure.</p>

<p>Dr. Ma served as Co-PI and lead developer of <strong>SimCCS 3.0</strong>, contributing to model development, large-scale network optimization, transport infrastructure analysis, storage integration, and decision-support applications.</p>

<p><strong>Funder:</strong> U.S. Department of Energy<br>
<strong>Role:</strong> Co-PI</p>

</div>

### National-Scale CCS Pipeline Modeling

<strong>Role:</strong> Co-PI</p>

### SMART: Science-Informed Machine Learning for Real-Time Subsurface Decision-Making

<div style="margin-left: 24px;">

<p>The SMART project developed <strong>science-informed machine-learning approaches to accelerate real-time decision-making in subsurface applications</strong>.</p>

<p>The research integrated physics-based simulation, machine learning, data assimilation, and uncertainty-aware modeling to improve computational efficiency and support faster, more informed subsurface decisions.</p>

<p><strong>Funder:</strong> U.S. Department of Energy<br>
<strong>Role:</strong> Co-Investigator</p>

</div>

---

### CATALOG: Consortium Advancing Technology for Assessment of Lost Oil & Gas Wells

<div style="margin-left: 24px;">

<p>CATALOG was a large-scale research consortium focused on developing advanced methods for <strong>identifying, characterizing, and assessing undocumented and orphaned oil and gas wells</strong>.</p>

<p>The project combined artificial intelligence, multimodal data analysis, geospatial methods, environmental assessment, and field information to improve understanding of legacy well infrastructure and associated environmental risks.</p>

<p><strong>Funder:</strong> U.S. Department of Energy<br>
<strong>Role:</strong> Work Package Lead</p>

</div>
