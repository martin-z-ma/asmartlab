The work includes techno-economic modelling, uncertainty and sensitivity analysis, machine-learning surrogate models, and development of a prototype web-based decision-support tool.

**Key areas:** CCUS · CO₂ maritime transport · Techno-economic analysis · Uncertainty quantification · Machine learning · Python
