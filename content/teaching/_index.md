Our teaching connects engineering fundamentals, data analytics, artificial intelligence, and applied problem-solving. Courses and student projects emphasize practical analysis, responsible use of computational tools, and communication of evidence-based engineering decisions.

## Current Teaching

### Data Analytics

An applied course introducing data preparation, statistical analysis, visualization, Excel, Python, and responsible AI applications through food-technology and engineering examples.

### Process Safety

Teaching contributions cover process hazards, release modeling, risk assessment, and safety management systems for pharmaceutical and chemical engineering students.

### AI Industry Team Project

Interdisciplinary student teams work with industry-defined challenges involving machine learning, natural language processing, and generative AI. The learning experience includes problem framing, feasibility assessment, responsible AI use, technical reporting, and industry presentation.

## Project-Based Learning

Student learning is supported through:

- Final-year projects
- Bachelor’s thesis projects
- Industry work-study projects
- Interdisciplinary AI team projects
- Applied data analytics assignments
- Research-led student supervision
