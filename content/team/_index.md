## Join A-SMART Lab

<div style="margin-left: 24px;">

<p>A-SMART Lab welcomes motivated researchers and students interested in <strong>AI-enabled engineering, scientific machine learning, optimization, uncertainty quantification, CCUS, geoenergy, and sustainable infrastructure systems</strong>.</p>

<p>Opportunities may include research staff positions, graduate research, final-year projects, bachelor thesis projects, internships, and visiting research collaborations.</p>
